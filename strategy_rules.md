## 1. Core Principles & Stock Selection Philosophy

As documented in Chapters 1 & 6 (Slides 3–15, 95–100):
1. **Universe Selection**:
   - Focus on Mid caps and Small caps with genuine liquidity.
   - Strictly avoid penny stocks and low-float stocks (e.g. >90% promoter holding, easily manipulated).
   - Look for **Former Runners**: stocks with a history of printing large green candles.
   - Look for stocks with catalysts / breaking news (earnings, acquisitions, order wins, sector tailwinds).
2. **Relative Volume (RVOL)**:
   - Volume is the "fuel/lifeline" of intraday breakouts. Patterns without abnormal volume are invalid.
   - Criterion: Stock trading at **2x to 3x** its average daily volume, or current day intraday volume substantially exceeding prior day volume.
3. **Daily Chart Confluence**:
   - Best setups align strong intraday price action with a strong daily chart:
     - Daily chart breaking resistance.
     - Daily chart crossing 52-week high with clean overhead room ("left and up windows").
     - Daily chart recovering from oversold conditions.
     - Trending daily chart supported by 8 EMA and 20 EMA.
     - Daily chart breaking round psychological numbers (₹100, ₹200, ₹500, ₹1000).

## 2. Technical Indicators Used

Explicitly defined in Chapter 2 (Slide 16):
- **8 EMA**: Short-term trend guide for trending markets.
- **20 EMA**: Medium-term dynamic support/resistance for trending markets.
- **Bollinger Bands (BB)**: 20-period, 2-std dev bands used for reversal setups.
- **Volume Bars**: Visual confirmation of volume expansion on breakout candles.

## 3. Explicit Intraday Strategies Documented in the Source

### Strategy Family A: Opening Strategies (Gap & Go Setups)

#### A.1: Previous Day High (PDH) Breakout Strategy (1-min Timeframe)
- **Timeframe**: 1-minute chart.
- **Setup**:
  - The first 1-minute candle of the day opens (can be within prior day's range) and **closes above the Previous Day High (PDH)**.
  - The first candle must exhibit **high relative volume**. Without high relative volume, the setup is **invalid**.
  - High probability variant: 1st candle is a Marubozu (strong solid body without excessive wicks, but not oversized/exhaustion).
- **Scanner**: Stock gainers with strong volume.
- **Entry**: Market / Limit entry on the close of the 1st 1-minute candle.
- **Stop Loss**: Placed strictly below the low of the 1st candle.
- **Exit**: Must close the trade between 09:30 and 10:00 AM IST.

#### A.2: First Pullback Strategy (5-min Timeframe)
- **Timeframe**: 5-minute chart.
- **Setup**:
  - Stock starts the day with an aggressive opening trend from the 1st candle.
  - Price pulls back / retraces to the confluence zone of the **8 EMA and 20 EMA**.
  - Confirmation trigger: Bullish pin bar (hammer/wick rejection) forming at the 8/20 EMA confluence.
  - Setup requires high relative volume for the day.
- **Scanner**: Stocks hitting new intraday highs with high RVOL.
- **Entry**: Close of the bullish pin bar off the 8 EMA / 20 EMA confluence.
- **Stop Loss**: Placed strictly below the 20 EMA.
- **Exit / Target**: Minimum 1:2 Risk-to-Reward ratio.

#### A.3: Red-to-Green Strategy (1-min or 5-min Timeframe)
- **Timeframe**: 1-min or 5-min chart.
- **Setup**:
  - Stock opens with a gap up above prior close.
  - The first candle is bearish (red candle) or prints a bearish pin bar / rejection.
  - Price consolidates and subsequently turns back upward ("Red to Green").
  - Confirmation: A strong green candle breaks and closes above the high of the opening red candle.
  - The breakout candle must have higher volume than preceding candles.
- **Scanner**: Stocks opening with gap up + high RVOL.
- **Entry**: Close of the green breakout candle above the opening candle high.
- **Stop Loss**: Placed strictly below the low of the breakout candle.

### Strategy Family B: Mid-Day Momentum Strategies

#### B.1: Volume Gainers Strategy (1-min or 5-min Timeframe)
- **Timeframe**: 1-min or 5-min chart.
- **Setup**: Mid-day / afternoon setup. Stock has been consolidating sideways in a narrow range (does not need to be trending prior).
- **Trigger**: Sudden massive volume spike prints a large bullish green candle out of consolidation.
- **Scanner**: Intraday volume gainers.
- **Entry**: Close of the volume spike candle.
- **Stop Loss**: Below the low of the entry candle.

#### B.2: Flat Top Breakout Strategy (1-min or 5-min Timeframe)
- **Timeframe**: 1-min or 5-min chart.
- **Setup**:
  - Stock is in a strong uptrend earlier in the session.
  - Enters an extended consolidation characterized by a series of small candles forming an equal horizontal resistance ceiling ("Flat Top") at the Day's High.
- **Trigger**: Candle breaks and closes above the Flat Top ceiling (new Day High).
- **Scanner**: Stocks trading at Day High with volume.
- **Entry**: Close of the breakout candle.
- **Stop Loss**: Below the low of the breakout candle.

#### B.3: 1-2-3-4 Momentum Strategy (5-min Timeframe)
- **Timeframe**: 5-minute chart (Common in Large Cap and F&O stocks).
- **Setup**:
  - Parabolic thrust establishes Point 1 (initial high).
  - Deep retracement establishes Point 2 (higher low support).
  - Bounce establishes Point 3 (lower high).
  - Point 4: Candle breaks above Point 3 resistance trendline.
- **Entry**: Candle breaking Point 3.
- **Stop Loss**: Below Point 4 / trendline invalidation.

#### B.4: Follow Through Day Strategy (5-min Timeframe)
- **Timeframe**: Daily chart context + 5-min execution.
- **Setup**:
  - Prior daily candle was a huge bullish green bar on massive volume breaking key resistance or rebounding from severe oversold.
  - Current day: 5-minute candle breaks above Previous Day High (PDH).
- **Trigger**: Breakout above PDH on 5-min chart.
- **Stop Loss**: Below the 5-min entry candle.

### Strategy Family C: Reversal Strategies

#### C.1: Fake Breakout Strategy (5-min Timeframe)
- **Timeframe**: Daily + 5-minute.
- **Setup**:
  - Prior day printed a massive breakout candle with heavy volume.
  - On the current day (or within 2-3 days), the breakout fails and price drops back below the breakout level.
- **Entry**: Short sell when a 5-min candle breaks and closes below the breakout line with volume.
- **Stop Loss**: Above the high of the entry candle.

#### C.2: 20 EMA Gap Down Strategy
- **Setup**: Trending stock that previously respected daily 20 EMA closes near/below 20 EMA. Next day opens gap down below Previous Day Low (PDL) and then reverses sharply back above PDL.
- **Entry**: Long entry when candle crosses and closes back above PDL.
- **Stop Loss**: Below the entry candle low.

#### C.3: Bollinger Band Gap Down Strategy
- **Setup**: Stock closes at or below lower 20-period Bollinger Band on Daily chart. Next day gaps down below PDL, reverses, and reclaims PDL.
- **Entry**: Long entry upon reclaiming PDL.
- **Stop Loss**: Below the entry candle low.

## 4. Money Management, Risk Control & Execution Rules

Explicitly documented in Chapter 8 (Trading Psychology & Plan for New Traders, Slides 112–117):
1. **Capital Risk**: Risk strictly **1% of trading capital** per trade.
2. **Trade Frequency Limit**: Maximum **2 trades per day**. System must shut down after 2 trades, regardless of whether they were winning or losing trades.
3. **Target / Risk-Reward Ratio**: Minimum **1:2 R:R** ratio.
4. **Position Scaling / Trailing**:
   - Move stop loss to **breakeven** once price travels **+1R** in favor.
   - Trail stop loss thereafter towards the 1:2 target.
5. **Execution Safety**:
   - Paper trade for a minimum of 2 months before committing live capital.
   - Strictly inspect Market Depth / Bid-Ask spread before executing; reject illiquid stocks or wide spreads (>0.1% spread proxy).
